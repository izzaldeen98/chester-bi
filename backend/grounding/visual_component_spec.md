# Filter specification

A filter element's `meta.filterRule.operator` must be copied verbatim from
its kind's list. `between`, `not between`, `last` and `next` take a
comma-joined `value` string (e.g. `2024-01-01 00:00:00,2024-12-31 00:00:00`).

## kind: "datetime"

- operators: `after`, `before`, `between`, `not between`, `next`, `last`, `equals`, `not equals`, `is null`, `is not null`

## kind: "date"

- operators: `after`, `before`, `between`, `not between`, `next`, `last`, `equals`, `not equals`, `is null`, `is not null`

## kind: "number"

- operators: `equals`, `not equals`, `greater than`, `greater than or equal to`, `less than`, `less than or equal to`, `between`, `not between`, `is any of`, `is null`, `is not null`

## kind: "text"

- operators: `equals`, `not equals`, `contains`, `not contains`, `starts with`, `not starts with`, `ends with`, `not ends with`, `is any of`, `is empty`, `is not empty`, `is null`, `is not null`
